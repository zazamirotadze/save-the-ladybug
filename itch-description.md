# Save the Ladybug: Fight for Freedom

🐞 A short, cozy browser puzzle game about guiding a tiny ladybug away from a cellar spider and toward the sunny window.

Place protective leaf barriers, tap the ladybug to turn it around, and survive ten increasingly dangerous levels. Can you save it from the cellar spider and reach freedom?

## Features

- 5 handcrafted levels: 2 story levels followed by 3 tougher Challenge levels
- Mouse and touch controls, with an optional Space/Enter shortcut to turn the ladybug around
- Unlocked levels saved locally in your browser
- Georgian and English localization
- Custom ladybug and spider colors
- Procedural sound effects, quiet menu music, and a cinematic epilogue, with separate music and sound controls in Settings
- Works directly in the browser — no installation required

## Controls

- Click/tap the wall: place a leaf barrier
- Click/tap the ladybug: turn it around
- `Space` / `Enter`: turn the ladybug around
- `Esc`: close a menu or return to the main menu

## Play time

The first run takes around 5–10 minutes. Replay the Challenge levels to master their narrow routes, moving spiders, and final cabinet obstacle.
